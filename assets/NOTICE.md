# Sound assets

## copy-pop.wav

Adapted from **Sharp Pop** by **CreatorsHome**, published April 17, 2025 on
[Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-sharp-pop-328170/).
Downloaded October 14, 2026. This sound is covered by the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/#license), not MIT.
See the [license summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).

Adaptation: decoded MP3 to 48 kHz mono 16-bit PCM, removed surrounding silence,
reduced peak level to prevent clipping, and applied 2 ms fades at the quiet ends.
Used as integrated clipboard feedback in Pano. Do not treat this file as an MIT
asset or redistribute it as standalone stock audio. The creator retains their
rights; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
